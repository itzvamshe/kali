## Practice Roadmap

After learning each topic:

* Learn the concept (30–60 min)
* Watch 2–3 example problems
* Solve **5 Easy** problems
* Solve **5 Medium** problems
* Revisit after one week

By the end of this roadmap, you'll have solved around **180–220 problems**, which provides a solid foundation for technical interviews and competitive programming.
